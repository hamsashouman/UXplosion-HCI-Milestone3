# UXplosion — Milestone 3 hierarchical task designs

Open `UXplosion-Six-HTDs.drawio` in diagrams.net. It contains six editable pages. Export each page to PDF or PNG for your submission.

## HTD 1: Student views exam results and personalized feedback

0. Student views exam results and personalized feedback

1. Open Student overview and click My exams.
2. Choose a teacher-released assessment. If pending, read its review status and stop.
3. Read the displayed final grade, question-level marks, and corrected answers.
4. Read the teacher-approved feedback and topic needing practice.
5. Read the recommended practice; click See my progress if desired.

**Plan 0:** Do 1–4 in order. Do 5 when a recommendation is available.

## HTD 2: Teacher verifies OCR-extracted answers

0. Teacher verifies OCR-extracted answers

1. Open Assessment review to access the current submitted exam.
2. Compare each original paper answer with its extracted text.
3. Edit any mismatched OCR text; repeat for all three questions.
4. Click Confirm answers & continue to unlock grade review.

**Plan 0:** Repeat 2–3 until the extracted answers are checked; then do 4.

## HTD 3: Teacher reviews and tracks evidence-based learning gaps

0. Teacher reviews and tracks evidence-based learning gaps

1. Open Students → Open learning profile, or Learning gaps.
2. Select Comparing fractions and read the linked questions and answers.
3. Compare the recurring misconception across the assessment timeline.
4. Tick the evidence-review acknowledgement checkbox after inspecting the evidence.
5. Confirm or dismiss the current learning-gap suggestion; separately review the support flag.
6. Read the updated evidence count and recurring/improving status.

**Plan 0:** Do 1–3, then acknowledge reviewed evidence in 4 before 5. Review the support flag separately. Confirming adds the current evidence; dismissing preserves previously confirmed evidence. Then do 6.

## HTD 4: Teacher plans and tracks learning-gap support plans

0. Teacher plans and tracks learning-gap support plans

1. From Learning gaps, click Open support plan.
2. Enter an improvement goal, measurable target, and recommended support activity.
3. Set a follow-up date and click Save support plan.
4. After the activity, check Support activity completed and click Save plan changes.
5. Review sample follow-up evidence and compare it with the measurable target.
6. Choose Ongoing, Successful, or Requires further support; enter notes and click Record outcome.

**Plan 0:** Do 1–3 to create the plan. At follow-up, do 4–6 in order.

## HTD 5: Teacher reviews and confirms AI grades

0. Teacher reviews and confirms AI grades

1. Open Assessment review after OCR text is confirmed.
2. Compare each verified student answer with its model answer and AI explanation.
3. Adjust the Final teacher mark field if the rubric requires a different score.
4. Repeat for every question; click Confirm grades & review learning gaps.
5. Review learning-gap and support-flag evidence and decisions; edit teacher feedback, then click Release results & notify student after all required reviews.

**Plan 0:** Repeat 2–3 for all questions before 4. Then complete the teacher decisions and feedback in 5 before releasing results; grades remain hidden until release.

## HTD 6: Student uploads an exam

0. Student uploads an exam

1. Click Upload an exam in Student view.
2. Enter the assessment title; choose Mathematics and the exam language (English or German).
3. Choose a supported image/PDF up to 10 MB, or Use a sample exam.
4. Click Preview exam and check the paper. If incorrect, click Choose another file and repeat 3–4.
5. Click Submit to my teacher.
6. Read the teacher-review status; return to My exams to check for released results.

**Plan 0:** Do 1–4 in order, including entering the assessment title. Correct an unsuitable file before 5. Then do 6.
